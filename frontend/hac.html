How It Works

From Registration to Rupees in 6
Simple Steps.

01 Login via Mobile

No complex crypto wallets. Simply enter your mobile number and OTP. We create a secure “Smart Account” for you instantly in the background.

02 Digital Identity

Your Aadhaar-linked profile is mapped to your wallet, creating a trusted on-chain identity for future services.

03 Record Harvest

Each sale you make at the Mandi is recorded on the blockchain as a tamper-proof income record.

04 Smart Verification

Lenders verify your harvest history instantly, without paperwork, enabling quick credit decisions.
