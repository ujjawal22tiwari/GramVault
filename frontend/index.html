How It Works

From Registration to Rupees in 6
Simple Steps.

01 Login via Mobile

No complex crypto wallets. Simply enter your mobile number and OTP. We create a secure “Smart Account” for you instantly in the background.

02 Digital Identity

The system initializes your "Soulbound Token" (SBT). This is your permanent, tamper-proof digital reputation on the Base Blockchain.

03 Record Harvest

When you sell your crop, the verified weight and price are recorded on-chain. This "Proof of Income" automatically boosts your credit limit.

04 Smart Verification

No bank visits. Our smart contracts analyze your harvest history and satellite weather data to calculate your exact loan eligibility in seconds.

05 Select Credit

Choose a loan offer (e.g., "Seeds & Fertilizer"). The funds are programmable and can only be spent at verified merchants, ensuring safety.

06 Instant Settlement

Funds arrive in your wallet instantly as e-Rupee tokens. Use them to pay vendors via QR code or withdraw to your bank account.
